# SpiderCaster turns words into websites.

You write what the page should say. SpiderCaster builds an elevated, self-contained website from your words and hands you the files. There is no database. There is nothing to log into. No monthly subscription fees. When the words change, you build again.

- Download SpiderCaster to start with one click.
- Automatic free agent seated in your browser studio.
- Instant local preview and one-click free staging.
- Keep every file. The site is yours forever.
