Everything on this list ships with the engine.

- Offline drafts: one command builds a site from your words. The draft costs nothing and needs no keys.
- Free agent seated in studio: the free model chain plans the site in the background with zero setup.
- One-click free staging: test on your port or share live online even before you own a domain.
- Small edits: change one section at a time instead of rebuilding the whole site.
- A strict checker: every page must pass every check with zero failures. One failure stops the job.
- Plain files out: the site is ordinary HTML and CSS with the style baked in. No database, no login, no upkeep.
- Room for seven seats: architect, content, frontend, graphics, visual critic, SEO, deployer. Each seat does its own job. None of them publish.
